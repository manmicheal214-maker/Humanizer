8. Mandatory Limitations & Ethical Statement

Probabilistic Context
  • AI detectors are probabilistic: Automated classification tools rely on statistical pattern matching and cannot conclusively prove authorship.
  • Perplexity is relative: Perplexity depends entirely on the language model evaluated and domain familiarity.
  • Human writing exhibits regular patterns: Academic, legal, technical, and non-native writing frequently exhibit low burstiness and formulaic transitions without being synthetic.
  • AI text can be instructed to vary: Prompts can instruct AI models to produce high burstiness and varied vocabulary.
  • Paraphrasing alters signatures: Editing and rewrites alter stylistic markers without changing factual veracity.
  • Stylometry is not proof: No computational analysis alone constitutes definitive proof of human or synthetic origin.
